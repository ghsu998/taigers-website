About Taigers Inc

A Sourcing Partner
You Can Rely On.

Taigers Inc is a California-based import trading company with established relationships with manufacturers across Asia. We work with U.S. businesses — from independent online sellers to established retail brands — to build reliable overseas supply chains.

Most U.S. buyers run into the same problems: unreliable suppliers, unexpected import costs, and no one accountable when things go wrong. We exist to solve exactly that. Our team understands the manufacturing environment in Asia and the regulatory requirements of importing into the United States — and we use that knowledge to protect your business at every step.

We are not a marketplace. We are not a broker. We are your dedicated import partner.

Why Work With Us

The Difference Is
In the Details.

01

Factory-Direct. Not Alibaba.

Most sourcing agents send your RFQ to whoever responds on a B2B marketplace. We don't. Our manufacturer relationships are built through years of direct business — verified track records, production capacity assessments, and in-person visits. You get a supplier who knows us and is accountable to us.

02

One Contact. Total Accountability.

Most importers juggle a factory, a freight forwarder, a customs broker, and a last-mile carrier — four separate parties, four separate invoices, and no one responsible for the full picture. With Taigers, one contact manages every step. When a problem arises, you make one call.

03

U.S. Import. Done Right.

Tariff classification, CBP documentation, HTS codes, duties estimation — import compliance is technical and costly when done wrong. We've navigated U.S. import regulations across product categories. Your shipments arrive on time, compliant, and without hidden charges.
